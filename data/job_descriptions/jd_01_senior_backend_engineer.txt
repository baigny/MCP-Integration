TITLE
Senior Backend Engineer

ABOUT
We are seeking an experienced Senior Backend Engineer to join our development team and help drive the growth of our scalable, cloud-based platform. As a key member of our team, you will be responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining our backend services, ensuring they are efficient, reliable, and secure.

RESPONSIBILITIES

* Design and develop high-performance, scalable backend services using Python and Django
* Collaborate with the DevOps team to implement containerization using Docker and orchestrate deployments using AWS
* Work with the database team to optimize PostgreSQL database performance and ensure data consistency
* Participate in code reviews, provide constructive feedback, and mentor junior engineers
* Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues, collaborating with cross-functional teams as needed
* Develop and maintain documentation for backend services, including API documentation and technical guides

REQUIREMENTS

* At least 5 years of experience in backend development with a strong focus on Python and Django
* Proficiency in PostgreSQL database management and performance optimization
* Experience with Docker and AWS, with a strong understanding of containerization and cloud-based deployment
* Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyze complex technical issues and develop effective solutions
* Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams

NICE TO HAVE

* Experience with asynchronous programming using libraries such as Celery or Zato
* Familiarity with message queues such as RabbitMQ or Apache Kafka
* Knowledge of containerization and orchestration tools such as Kubernetes or Apache Mesos
* Experience with continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines using tools like Jenkins or GitLab CI/CD
* Strong understanding of cloud security best practices and compliance requirements for AWS
