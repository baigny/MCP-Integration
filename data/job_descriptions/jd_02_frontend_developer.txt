TITLE
Senior Frontend Developer

ABOUT
We're seeking an experienced Frontend Developer to join our team in the development of our flagship product, a cutting-edge e-commerce platform. As a key member of our team, you will be responsible for building and maintaining high-performance, scalable, and user-friendly web applications using the latest technologies.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Collaborate with our design team to bring visually stunning and intuitive UI/UX designs to life
- Develop and maintain high-quality, maintainable, and efficient frontend code using React, JavaScript, and TypeScript
- Write clean, modular, and well-documented code that follows our company's coding standards
- Work closely with our backend team to ensure seamless integration with our RESTful APIs
- Troubleshoot and resolve complex frontend issues in a timely and efficient manner
- Participate in code reviews and contribute to the improvement of our codebase
- Stay up-to-date with the latest frontend trends, technologies, and best practices

REQUIREMENTS
- At least 2 years of experience as a Frontend Developer
- Proficiency in React, JavaScript, TypeScript, and CSS
- Strong understanding of web development principles, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Experience with TypeScript and its ecosystem
- Familiarity with CSS preprocessors like Sass or Less
- Experience with frontend build tools like Webpack or Rollup
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with state management libraries like Redux or MobX
- Familiarity with testing frameworks like Jest or Enzyme
- Knowledge of containerization using Docker
- Experience with cloud-based services like AWS or Google Cloud
- Strong understanding of accessibility guidelines and best practices
