TITLE
Senior Full Stack Developer

ABOUT
We're seeking a seasoned Full Stack Developer to join our team of innovators in creating cutting-edge digital solutions for our clients. As a Senior Full Stack Developer, you will play a pivotal role in architecting, designing, and developing scalable and efficient web applications using the latest technologies. If you're passionate about coding, problem-solving, and collaborative work, we'd love to hear from you.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable, and high-performance web applications using React, Node.js, and MongoDB.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify project requirements and provide technical expertise.
- Develop and implement data models, APIs, and schema using GraphQL.
- Conduct thorough code reviews, ensure adherence to coding standards, and participate in the continuous improvement of our codebase.
- Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues, identifying and implementing efficient solutions.
- Participate in the evaluation and implementation of new technologies to enhance our development workflow.

REQUIREMENTS
- At least 3 years of experience in software development, preferably in a Full Stack role.
- Proficiency in React, Node.js, MongoDB, and GraphQL.
- Strong understanding of front-end and back-end development principles.
- Experience with Agile development methodologies, including Scrum and Kanban.
- Excellent problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently and collaboratively.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with cloud-based services such as AWS or Azure.
- Familiarity with containerization using Docker.
- Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automated testing.
- Experience with security best practices and threat modeling.
- Strong understanding of DevOps principles and practices.
